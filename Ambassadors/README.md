Welcome to the **NTHW Ambassador Program**! This initiative empowers passionate individuals to lead mini-projects, create educational content, and foster our cybersecurity community under the NTHW banner.


## Who Are Our Ambassadors?

NTHW Ambassadors are experienced cybersecurity professionals, enthusiasts, and educators who:

- Represent the NTHW project in their local and online communities  
- Lead mini-projects such as workshops, tool development, or educational series  
- Mentor newcomers, moderate forums, and assist with technical questions  
- Create blog posts, tutorials, webinars, and presentations  
- Organize or speak at conferences, meetups, and capture-the-flag events  

Ambassadors enjoy direct support from the core team, collaboration opportunities on open source tools, and visibility across our channels.

## Ambassador Profiles

Each ambassador has a dedicated profile page under the `Ambassadors/People/` directory, showcasing:

- Country  
- Contact information (email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Twitter, Discord)  
- Biography and cybersecurity background  
- Technical competencies and interests  
- Planned initiatives and “Available for” checklist  
- Language proficiency and additional skills  
- Long-term vision for community impact  

Browse existing ambassadors here:

## How to Apply

Interested in becoming an NTHW Ambassador? We’d love to hear from you! Please send your application to **miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com** with the following information:

1. **Why you want to become an NTHW Ambassador**  
2. **What unique skills and experience you bring**  
3. **Ideas for mini-projects or initiatives** you’d like to lead  
4. **Availability and preferred areas of involvement** (e.g., content creation, mentoring, event organization)  
5. **Links to your online presence** (GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter, blog, etc.)  

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Qualified candidates will be invited for a brief interview and asked to complete a short onboarding task.

## Getting Started

1. Send an email to [miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com](mailto:miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com) - with topic name: **Ambassador Program - Your Name** explaining your motivation and qualifications.
2. After a positive response, you will receive a profile template to complete. 
3. Fill out the template and reply to the email with your completed file.
4. Join our Discord community for real-time discussions and support: [https://discord.gg/fjwzWFWuzg](https://discord.gg/fjwzWFWuzg)
