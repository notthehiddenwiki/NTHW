## 👤 Biography
I am passionate about cybersecurity and cyber threat intelligence, and my goal is to support the community in gaining knowledge about privacy, safety, and responsible use of online resources.

I hold a master's degree from the Campus Internacional de Ciberseguridad in Spain. Currently, I am developing **Gamely** – a cyber threat intelligence platform dedicated to small and medium-sized enterprises that often lack access to specialized tools and expertise. Gamely translates threat intelligence into actionable reports and was created to provide essential knowledge and promote a proactive attitude in smaller organizations.

I believe I can contribute to NTHW by promoting valuable resources and educating others in OSINT and cyber intelligence.

## 🛠️ Technical Competencies and Interests
**Cyber Threat Intelligence:** I work with the OpenCTI platform, integrating data from MITRE ATT&CK, Malpedia, AlienVault, and other sources of enrichment. But knowing is just half the battle, so I transform the information gathered through the platform is transformed into actionable reports.

**Mentoring:** I have conducted cybersecurity training for freelancers, SMEs and public administration.

**Educational Content Creation:** I publish educational materials, guides, and presentations related to cyber intelligence.
