## 👤 Biography
I am a cybersecurity and cloud security specialist with several years of experience in personal data protection and security breach analysis, including both GDPR-related incidents and broader IT security incidents. I graduated from the National Defence University in Warsaw with a degree in Information Security.

After completing my studies, I spent several years working in information security at Medicover, where I was responsible for maintaining and managing information security breach registers across multiple Medicover companies. My role also involved coordinating information security incident response processes and ensuring compliance with data protection regulations.

After several years of working in information security and incident coordination, I decided to further develop my career in cybersecurity. Following six months of intensive self-education, I joined a Security Operations Center as an L1 analyst. Shortly thereafter, I advanced to the L2 role and was later appointed SOC Coordinator, where I was responsible for overseeing operations and supporting incident response activities.

Since November 2025, I have been working as a Cybersecurity Consultant at a company specializing in the implementation and maintenance of security systems. In my daily work, I continuously strive to expand my knowledge and skills to improve my professional qualifications. Outside of work, I regularly read industry literature, follow the latest trends in security technologies, and deepen my expertise in cybersecurity. I also gain additional knowledge and hands-on experience by participating in courses, training sessions, and webinars organized by Sekurak, Niebezpiecznik, Cisco, TCM Security, and similar organizations.

Privately, I am a multi-instrumentalist musician, motorcyclist, and video editor. I enjoy traveling the world on a motorcycle, playing good rock music, and capturing these experiences through film.
As an NTHW ambassador, I will focus on sharing practical knowledge, promoting security awareness, and contributing to the community through educational content, mentoring, and collaboration.
