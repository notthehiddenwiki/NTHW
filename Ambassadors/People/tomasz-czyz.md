## 👤 Biography
I am an IT engineer, born in Poland but living in Norway. I have been a computer enthusiast since I got my first PC in 1991. While my peers were gaming, I was more curious about how computers and applications work from the inside. I am a graduate of the University of Bielsko-Biala with a degree in Computer Science, focusing on network and database administration. I have worked in many different IT areas, including programming, IT support at levels 2 and 3, server, backup, network, and cloud administration. Since 2015, my focus has mainly been on the technical side of cybersecurity (not the GRC part).

Currently, I am working as a consultant at one of the biggest IT companies in the Nordics. I mainly work with penetration testing and vulnerability assessments and am also a member of the incident response team.

In my free time, I like to run, spend time with my family, play CTFs, and test things in my lab.

I discovered the NTHW project not so long ago and love the idea of sharing knowledge and learning from others within a community, so I joined to contribute and help it grow.
