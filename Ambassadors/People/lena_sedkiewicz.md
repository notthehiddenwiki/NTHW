## 👤 Biography
I'm currently an Intern Software Engineer at Bayer - a psychologist who decided to become a programmer and cybersecurity specialist. This unusual path means I approach security from two angles at once: the human side, shaped by psychology, and the technical side, grounded in software engineering. Together with Adam Behan I contributed to Robert Kruczek's book _Socjotechnika w praktyce_, published by Securitum. I'm also a speaker, an occasional lecturer, and a blogger.

My goal as an ambassador is to give back through practical, accessible educational content and to bring the human perspective - how people actually behave, not only how systems are built - into conversations that are often purely technical. Outside of work I'm a fan of Formula 1, gardening, and crocheting.

## 🛠️ Technical Competencies and Interests
On the engineering side I work primarily with TypeScript, React, Node.js, and JavaScript, with Python in my toolkit as well. My security interests centre on application security and social engineering - areas where my two backgrounds meet most naturally.

What I bring is an unusual combination: a background in psychology paired with software engineering, with cybersecurity running through both. That mix lets me reason about security from the human side as well as the technical one - thinking about how people actually behave under manipulation or error, not just about how a system is implemented. Having co-authored a chapter on social engineering, I'm especially interested in the point where technical vulnerabilities and human factors overlap.
