## 👤 Biography
I'm Ahmed Amine Derbali, known online as serioton. I'm a graduated cybersecurity student, and I got into the field back in 2021 through HackTheBox machines and CTF competitions, both local and international. I'm currently ranked 10th worldwide and 1st in Tunisia on HackTheBox.

I want to become an NTHW ambassador because I care about giving back to the community. I want to help others, exchange knowledge with people more skilled than me, and keep learning in the process. I also want to bring NTHW to my local community, to motivate and inspire people around me to improve and collaborate with each other. I'm planning to contribute the cheatsheets I've written, publish articles on techniques and tools I pick up along the way, and be an active part of growing the NTHW community.

## 🛠️ Technical Competencies and Interests

My core focus is Active Directory security. I'm especially interested in attack path analysis, Kerberos abuse, ADCS misconfigurations, and lateral movement techniques.

Beyond AD, I'm active in CTF competitions across multiple categories, including forensics, cryptography, pwn, web exploitation, reverse engineering, and misc challenges. I also build my own security tooling as a way to deepen my understanding of the techniques I study, ranging from red team utilities to CTF-focused tools.

I'm also working my way into vulnerability research, studying Windows internals. I care about not just using existing tools, but understanding how they work well enough to build my own, and eventually find new attack vectors myself.
