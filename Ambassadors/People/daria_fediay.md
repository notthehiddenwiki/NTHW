## 👤 Biography

Daria Fediay is the Chief Executive Officer and co-founder of CypSec, a European cybersecurity company focused on SaaS security ecosystems, including vulnerability management, active defense, and secure communications. She combines a background in business strategy and information security with extensive experience in operational management, international partnerships, and go-to-market strategies for technology-driven organizations.

Her interest in cybersecurity originates from a belief that security innovation must be accessible and collaborative. Through CypSec, she has worked on integrating automation, policy-as-code, and active defense concepts to bridge the gap between corporate governance and technical protection.

As an NTHW Ambassador, Daria aims to strengthen collaboration between open communities and established cybersecurity firms, supporting initiatives that improve digital resilience, promote secure development practices, and encourage ethical hacking as a driver of innovation.

## 🛠️ Technical Competencies and Interests
