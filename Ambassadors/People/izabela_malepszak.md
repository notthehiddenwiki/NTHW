## 👤 Biography

In my daily work as the Management Board’s Representative for Integrated Management Systems, I am responsible for a holistic approach to information security within the organization. I hold ISO Internal Auditor certifications (27001, 22301, 27018) as well as the title of Cybersecurity Representative for Key and Important Entities, in line with NIS 2, PN-EN ISO/IEC 27001:2023-08, PN-EN ISO 22301:2020-04, and GDPR requirements.

My passion for cybersecurity extends far beyond documentation. I regularly participate in major industry conferences such as Confidence, OhMyHack, The Hack Summit, and CyberTrust. I place particular emphasis on the intersection of law and technology, which I continue to explore at events like the DORA Forum and Puls Biznesu seminars. I am especially interested in how emerging regulations influence IT system architecture and the practical aspects of digital resilience.

Privately, I’m a person of many passions that fuel my creativity. I’ve been loyal to darker sounds for years - my headphones are ruled by EBM, IDM, Industrial, Cold Wave, Techno, and electroclash. If there’s a good party in town, that’s probably where you’ll find me. Those who know me can easily recognize me by the glitter and unicorns.

I’m also a photography enthusiast and an admirer of extremely cold climates - Iceland and Norway are my places on Earth, and my dream is to visit Greenland and Lapland. I split my free time between good cinema (I’ve been hooked on anime since childhood), used to be an avid bookworm, and remain a devoted cat lover. I also have a soft spot for all the “scary” animals that others often overlook - spiders and bats included. My long-time passion for swimming means I often feel more at home in water than on land.
