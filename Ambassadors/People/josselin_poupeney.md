## 👤 Biography
I'm Joss Poupeney a french penetration tester based in Brazil. I'm a self-taught hacker, I learned everything from amazing platforms such as Hack The Box, PortSwigger, and from awesome people sharing their knowledge online. Finding resources wasn't easy because I didn't know where to search, if I had the NTHW back then, it would have saved me so much time, which is why I now want to help the project by becoming an ambassador. 

I've worked as a Security Engineer, Penetration Tester, and I'm currently a Cybersecurity Instructor at Evolve Security Academy. My interests are mostly towards offensive security, the problem-solving mindset it need is probably my favorite part. When I'm doing a CTF on Hack The Box or any platform, this is like I'm playing a new video game.

## 🛠️ Technical Competencies and Interests
My main interests are Web security and Active Directory. I did extended research on AS-REP Roasting and wrote a blog post about it on Medium. I like practicing on platforms but also building my own labs (AD and web app) so I can see things from both, attacker and defender perspective. In web security, my favorite part is reconnaissance, taking the time to map a target manually and finding new subdomains.

Recently like a lot of people in the field, I started diving into AI security, not only how to break AI systems but how to use AI as part of my workflow. I'm currently training Claude with custom skills to act as a bug bounty hunting partner.

As a cybersecurity instructor, one of my biggest passions is giving back, especially to beginners. I have a lot of patience with people just starting out and I can breaking down complex topics into easy to understand.
