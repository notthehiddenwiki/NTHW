# 🧑‍💼 Not The Hidden Employees - Job Board

## 📋 What is NTHE Job Board?

NTHE Job Board is an GitHub-based idea that connects employers with IT candidates in a completely anonymous way (no risk of personal data sharing with forks). Candidates create profiles with their skills and job preferences while maintaining full privacy of their personal data.

## 🎯 How it works?

### For candidates:
1. **Create a profile** - copy the `template-person.md` template and customize it
2. **Name your file** - use format `NTHE_person-XXX.md` where XXX is a unique number (e.g. `NTHE_person-001.md`)
3. **Submit Pull Request** - add your profile to the `/profiles/` folder (If you want, you can send your profile to [miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com](mailto:miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com))
4. **Stay anonymous** - don't include any personal data

### For employers:
1. **Browse profiles** in the `/profiles/` folder
2. **Find interesting candidates** based on their skills and preferences
3. **Contact us** - send email to: **[miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com](mailto:miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com)** with profile number
4. **Get contact** - after verification, we'll pass contact to the candidate

## 🛡️ Privacy and security

- **Full anonymity** - no personal data is published
- **Controlled contact** - all inquiries go through administrator
- **Safe forks** - no risk of personal data leakage with forks
- **Voluntary** - candidate decides whether to accept contact with given company

## 📝 Participation rules

### For candidates:
- ✅ Use only the `template-person.md` template
- ✅ Don't provide any personal data (name, surname, email, phone, address) - The magic of forks can cause your personal data to circulate on GitHub.
- ✅ Be honest about your skills
- ✅ Update your profile regularly
- ❌ Don't duplicate profiles
- ❌ Don't add links to your social media profiles

### For employers:
- ✅ Respect candidate anonymity
- ✅ Use profile number in communication
- ✅ Be specific about the offered position
- ❌ Don't try to identify candidates outside the system
- ❌ Don't spam with inquiries

## 📋 FAQ

**Q: Can I have more than one profile?**
A: No, one candidate = one profile.

**Q: How to update my profile?**
A: Submit a Pull Request with updates or write to [miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com](mailto:miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com).

**Q: Can I delete my profile?**
A: Yes, write to [miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com](mailto:miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com) with deletion request - Your profile will remain in old commits. Therefore, do not publish personal data.

**Q: Can I skip parts of the template that I don't want to fill in?**
A: Of course, there is no problem if you skip a section. Just remember to delete any empty sections so that your future employer has easier access to the information.

**Q: I have an idea for improving the employee template. How do I report it?**
A: You can make a Pull Request or email us.
