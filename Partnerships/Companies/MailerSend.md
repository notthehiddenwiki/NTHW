### Bio

MailerSend is an innovative transactional messaging service built for developers to quickly integrate reliable email delivery into their app or website. It offers a robust API, SDKs in 7 languages, SMTP relay, intuitive template builders, and more, ensuring a flexible, team-friendly solution that makes it easy to send, receive and manage transactional messages at scale.

The company’s core focus is to simplify and streamline transactional communications, and its continued growth proves that advanced programmatic email delivery can be achieved without the need for complex infrastructure, steep learning curves, or excessive developer overhead.

With its cutting-edge technology, and team of experts committed to continuous product development and customer support, MailerSend has established itself as a key player in the rapidly growing communications sector. By combining ease of use with advanced functionality and exceptional deliverability, it empowers businesses to build strong relationships with their customers through effective and dependable transactional communication.
