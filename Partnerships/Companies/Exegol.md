### Bio

Exegol is a versatile, container-based cybersecurity environment tailored for offensive security professionals, red teamers, penetration testers, and security researchers. Developed by a community of ethical hackers and security experts, Exegol addresses the common frustrations found in traditional security distributions by offering a modern, reliable, and highly customizable toolkit.<br><br>
Unlike bloated or outdated platforms, Exegol is designed to be lightweight, reproducible, and easy to maintain. It leverages the power of Docker to deliver a consistent environment that can be quickly deployed, updated, and extended — whether you’re conducting red teaming operations, vulnerability research or training exercises.<br><br>
At its core, Exegol emphasizes flexibility and modularity. Its plugin system allows users to easily add or remove tools, ensuring only relevant capabilities are included for each engagement. Backed by an active and growing community, Exegol continues to evolve rapidly, incorporating cutting-edge tools, scripts, and workflows based on real-world feedback and operational needs.<br><br>
Whether you're working in the field, preparing for a CTF, or running a complex adversary simulation, Exegol provides a stable and efficient platform that scales with your expertise.

Have you ever:
- Struggled to keep your distro running smoothly after a few months?
- Wasted hours installing and configuring tools instead of doing actual security work?
- Felt limited by outdated or lacking tools in traditional security distributions?

Exegol addresses these challenges!
