### Bio

Inside Darknet is a German podcast that explores the hidden layers of the internet, the underground economy, and the people living inside it. Each episode pulls listeners directly into real cases of cybercrime, hacking, malware development, phishing operations, and darknet marketplaces - often told by the very people who are still active in that world.

Since its launch in 2023, the podcast has released over 80 episodes, with new content every Saturday on all major streaming platforms. Episodes are mixing investigations, raw conversations, and firsthand interviews with hackers, researchers, and underground operators.

German listeners can join the Inside Darknet community on Discord, where enthusiasts and professionals discuss episodes, share knowledge, and stay updated on the latest cybercrime trends.

Inside Darknet stands out because it doesn’t just analyze stories from the outside - it brings unfiltered voices from the underground itself. The mission is to make the digital underworld accessible and thought-provoking, offering defenders, researchers, and curious minds an authentic look into the lives, methods, and mindsets shaping today’s cybercrime scene.
