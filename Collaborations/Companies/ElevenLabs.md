### Bio

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology. Their vision is to make communication and creation with technology seamless - 
starting with the first human-like voice model and now expanding well beyond voice.<br><br>
ElevenLabs builds three platforms: **ElevenAgents** enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and 
reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale. **ElevenCreative** empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ 
languages. **ElevenAPI** gives developers access to their leading AI audio foundational models.<br><br>
Through the **ElevenLabs Impact program**, ElevenLabs provides free licenses to those who need it most — including individuals with accessibility needs and nonprofit organizations 
across healthcare, education, and culture.<br><br>
AI safety is inseparable from innovation at ElevenLabs. They maintain a dedicated safety team combining research, engineering, and policy, deploying a comprehensive set of 
safeguards in a multi-layered defense system to prevent, detect, enforce, and inform against misuse.<br><br>
Everything ElevenLabs builds is the result of the creativity and commitment of their team - researchers, engineers, and operators, IOI medalists and ex-founders - all doing the best 
work of their lives and creating lasting positive impact.
