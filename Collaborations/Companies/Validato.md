### Bio

Validato is a Swiss-based background check and identity verification platform purpose-built for organizations that treat human risk as a strategic priority. The platform combines diploma verification, criminal record checks, reference validation, and continuous vetting into a single, audit-ready workflow - covering candidates and employees across more than 190 countries. <br>

With a human-in-the-loop approach at its core, Validato ensures that automated processes are always backed by qualified analysts - delivering not just data, but verified, actionable intelligence. The platform is built for compliance from the ground up, meeting the requirements of GDPR, Swiss revDSG, ISO 27001, and sector-specific frameworks such as DIN SPEC 14027. <br>

Validato operates entirely within European infrastructure, making it the trusted alternative for organizations that require independence from US-based cloud providers and legal jurisdictions. From pre-employment screening to ongoing Human Risk Management, Validato gives security teams the visibility they need - where risk begins: with people.
