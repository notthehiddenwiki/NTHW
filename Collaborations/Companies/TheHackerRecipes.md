### Bio

This project is dedicated to delivering high-quality, technical guides on a wide range of hacking and offensive security topics. Its primary focus lies in the deep exploration of Active Directory and Web services, which are among the most complex and frequently targeted components in real-world environments.<br><br>
Unlike traditional cheatsheets, which tend to either oversimplify or overwhelm, this project takes a different approach. While quick references have their place, they often lack critical context, explanations, or actionable depth. This is not a cheatsheet repository.<br><br>
Instead, the majority of the content here is original, thoroughly researched, and tested in practical scenarios whenever possible. The goal is to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and hands-on application — helping readers not just repeat commands, but understand why and how things work under the hood.<br><br>
Whether you're a red teamer, pentester, security researcher, or just a curious learner, you'll find detailed walkthroughs, practical examples, and insight into offensive techniques that go beyond surface-level guides.<br><br>
This is a living, evolving collection — built by hackers, for hackers.
