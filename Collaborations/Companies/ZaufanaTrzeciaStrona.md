### Bio

Zaufana Trzecia Strona is a leading, independent Polish online portal focused on IT security, privacy, and data protection. For years, it has been building trust among industry professionals, businesses, public institutions, and everyday internet users. The platform was created to provide reliable and verified information in a time when cyber threats are becoming increasingly common and complex. What sets Z3S apart is its editorial independence, in-depth analysis, and ability to respond quickly to current incidents – from high-profile data leaks to vulnerabilities exploited in real-world attacks. The portal’s creators are also active members of both the Polish and European cybersecurity communities.<br><br>
The site regularly publishes news, commentary, and deep-dive analyses on digital threats, hacking incidents, software vulnerabilities, data breaches, and privacy-related events. One of its flagship initiatives is the “Weekendowa Lektura” – a weekly roundup of the most important global cybersecurity stories, carefully curated and enriched with expert commentary. Z3S covers a broad spectrum of topics – from technical deep dives to legal issues, social engineering, mobile security, and the broader social consequences of cybercrime. All content is organized by categories and tags, making it easy for readers to find what interests them most.<br><br>
Alongside the Polish-language portal, the team also runs BadCyber, an English-language platform aimed at the global cybersecurity community. BadCyber focuses on delivering the most relevant infosec news in a concise, accessible format – often in the form of summaries, rankings, and expert breakdowns, such as the popular “IT Security Weekend Catch Up.” Together, Zaufana Trzecia Strona and BadCyber form a synergistic ecosystem – one of the few Polish initiatives that successfully bridges domestic expertise with an international perspective. Their content reaches a wide range of readers, from administrators and developers to journalists, academics, and decision-makers concerned with cyber risk.<br><br>
