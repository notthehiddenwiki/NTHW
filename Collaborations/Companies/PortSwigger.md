### Bio

PortSwigger is a leading British cybersecurity company best known as the creator of Burp Suite, the world's most widely used web application security testing tool. Trusted by security professionals, penetration testers, and bug bounty hunters across the globe, Burp Suite provides a comprehensive toolkit for discovering and exploiting vulnerabilities in web applications - from intercepting HTTP traffic to automated scanning and advanced manual testing techniques.

Beyond their flagship product, PortSwigger is deeply committed to security education through the Web Security Academy - a free, hands-on learning platform offering hundreds of labs, detailed tutorials, and real-world scenarios covering the full spectrum of web vulnerabilities, including SQL injection, XSS, SSRF, authentication flaws, business logic vulnerabilities, and much more. The Academy is widely regarded as one of the best free resources in the industry, used by both beginners and seasoned professionals looking to sharpen their offensive and defensive skills.

PortSwigger's research team continuously pushes the boundaries of web security, publishing cutting-edge vulnerability research and contributing to the broader security community through conferences, whitepapers, and open knowledge sharing. Their work has helped shape modern web security standards and testing methodologies adopted worldwide.

With a mission to make the web more secure, PortSwigger empowers security professionals at every stage of their career - from students taking their first steps in ethical hacking to expert researchers uncovering zero-days in enterprise environments.
