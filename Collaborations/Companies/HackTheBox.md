### Bio

Hack The Box (HTB) is the number one leading platform for building high-performing cyber teams and organizations prepared to combat real-world cyber threats. Founded with the philosophy of being "Community Inspired. Enterprise Trusted", HTB has become the reference standard for cybersecurity training, talent recruitment, and technical competency validation across the global industry.

Hack The Box addresses a critical challenge in the industry: more than 50% of significant cyber incidents are caused by lack of skills or human error. The platform was designed to close this knowledge gap and prepare versatile 360-degree cyber professionals who understand both offensive and defensive security perspectives. Its objective is to develop market-ready professionals capable of identifying, exploiting, detecting, and mitigating vulnerabilities in real enterprise infrastructure.

#### Structure and Main Applications
HTB operates through four integrated applications, each focused on a specific aspect of cybersecurity skill development:

**HTB Main Application** is the heart of the platform, where real virtual machines are deployed for users to practice penetration testing. Users solve interactive security challenges, from reconnaissance and exploitation to post-exploitation, in a completely realistic environment. Machines are continuously updated with the latest technologies and attack vectors.

**HTB Academy** offers a structured educational approach with guided courses, thematic modules, and industry-recognized certifications. The platform includes more than 145 specialized modules covering everything from fundamentals to advanced techniques, with over 1,200 interactive labs. Courses use a "threat-informed" methodology based on real-world examples, and students can practice everything directly in the browser using Pwnbox, an online penetration testing virtual machine that requires no additional infrastructure configuration.

**HTB CTF Platform** is the dedicated platform for Capture The Flag competitions, where players can participate in competitive challenges both individually and in teams. CTFs are excellent for rapid skills assessment, development of lateral thinking, and community building among security professionals.

**HTB Threat Range** represents the company's most recent innovation: a team-based live-fire simulation that tests SOC and DFIR (Digital Forensics and Incident Response) capabilities under realistic cyber attack scenarios. It allows defensive teams to sharpen detection and investigation, establish performance benchmarks with real security metrics, and break down silos between departments through collaborative gameplay.

#### World-Class Educational Content

The platform provides dynamic and continuously updated content, with new research-focused labs addressing emerging technologies and attack vectors launching every week. Modules cover both fundamental skills (Linux, Windows, Networking, Python, Bash) and advanced techniques (DACL Attacks, ADCS Attacks, NTLM Relay, Supply Chain Attacks, AI Red Teaming, LLM Injection Attacks).

The educational approach is highly practical: all labs run against real applications and real infrastructure, allowing students to gain valuable direct experience. This contrasts sharply with platforms that offer only simulations.

Hack The Box has established the gold standard for practical cybersecurity training, surpassing competitors in technical variety, challenge depth, and relevance to modern threats. The combination of interactive hacking platform, structured academy, CTF competitions, and defensive simulations creates a unique ecosystem that prepares professionals for any cybersecurity role in the real world.
