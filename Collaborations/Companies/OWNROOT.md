### Bio

**OWNROOT** is an AI‑native cybersecurity platform that unifies **GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance)**, **ASM (Attack Surface Management)**, and **PTaaS (Penetration Testing as a Service)** into a single ecosystem. The platform is designed specifically for the European regulatory landscape, helping organizations turn compliance requirements (e.g., AI Act, DORA, NIS2, CRA) into daily, measurable security actions.

OWNROOT brings together three pillars:

- **Security** – real protection against threats,  
- **Compliance** – alignment with regulations and standards,  
- **Action** – concrete, measurable actions and evidence.

Instead of another dashboard, you get a **platform that enforces closing risks** and generates evidence useful for auditors, management, and customers. 

### Built by the team behind NTHW

**OWNROOT is built by the team behind NTHW.**  
Not The Hidden Wiki (NTHW) focuses on **cybersecurity education for individuals** – learning paths, materials, CTFs, and community.  
**OWNROOT** is a **separate product for organizations** – a platform that helps security and compliance teams implement, maintain, and prove regulatory compliance in day‑to‑day operations.

**Same roots, different audience.**
