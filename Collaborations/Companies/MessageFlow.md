### Bio
MessageFlow is an omnichannel communication platform for managing both marketing and transactional messaging through a single, unified API. The platform seamlessly integrates SMS, Email, Push, RCS, and Viber, enabling real-time, personalized communication at scale.

Designed for high performance, MessageFlow delivers 99.98% message deliverability, sub-second routing, and carrier-grade reliability. Its infrastructure processes millions of messages per hour and leverages direct operator connectivity to maintain low latency and consistent delivery quality.

Security and compliance are core to the platform. MessageFlow adheres to ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27018, and ISO/IEC 22301 standards and meets the requirements of GDPR, NIS2, and DORA, ensuring robust data protection across all communication flows.

As part of the Vercom Group, MessageFlow supports global brands such as Rossmann, IKEA, Victoria’s Secret, ING Bank, DPD, and Douglas, delivering reliable, scalable communication in more than 180 countries.

One secure API. All channels. Enterprise-grade communication without compromise.
