## How to Join the NTHW-12 Mini School for Aspiring Pentesters

-  **Who Can Apply?**  
   The mini school is designed for enthusiasts who already have **basic knowledge of web applications** (such as HTTP protocols, HTML structure, and common security vulnerabilities). We do **not teach fundamentals from scratch**, so candidates need this foundational knowledge before joining.

-  **How to Express Interest?**  
   Send an email to [miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com](mailto:miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com) with the subject line **[nthw-12-mini-school]**.

-  **What Information to Provide?**  
   Include your full name, current education or experience level, and a brief motivation statement explaining your interest in pentesting and what you hope to learn.

-  **Assessment Step**  
   You may be asked to complete a short quiz or security challenge to demonstrate your current skills and readiness for the program.

-  **Collaborative Learning with Experts**  
   Participants will conduct pentests working alongside experienced pentesters and red team members, gaining hands-on mentorship and practical experience.

-  **Commitment Requirements**  
   The mini school requires dedication to participate in workshops, labs, and team projects over a defined period.

-  **Community and Communication**  
   Accepted applicants will join a dedicated Discord channels for collaboration, mentorship, and ongoing learning.

-  **Recognition**  
   Participants receive the opportunity to log practical pentesting experience on their CVs.

---

We are excited to mentor the next generation of cybersecurity professionals and provide real-world experience in a supportive environment.
