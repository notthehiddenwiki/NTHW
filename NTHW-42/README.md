## 🚀 Quick Overview

**NTHW-42 Unit** is a revolutionary initiative that brings together **20+ elite cybersecurity specialists** to provide **world-class penetration testing and red team operations completely free of charge**.

In an era where regulations like **NIS2** and **DORA** demand robust cybersecurity, many organizations struggle with the high costs of professional security assessments. We bridge this gap by democratizing access to enterprise-grade security testing.

### ⚡ What We Offer
- **Professional Penetration Testing** across web, network, and cloud environments
- **Advanced Red Team Operations** simulating real-world adversaries
- **Comprehensive Security Reports** with actionable remediation guidance
- **Zero Cost** - completely free, no hidden fees or future obligations

### 🎯 Our Mission
**Cybersecurity should be a standard, not a commodity.** We make professional security testing accessible to all organizations, regardless of budget, while providing real-world experience to emerging security professionals.

We believe that cybersecurity expertise should be accessible to all organizations, regardless of their budget. Our initiative brings together elite security professionals who donate their time and expertise to:

- **Democratize cybersecurity** by removing financial barriers to professional security testing
- **Elevate industry standards** through high-quality, comprehensive security assessments
- **Foster talent development** by providing real-world experience to emerging security professionals
- **Strengthen the overall security posture** of the digital ecosystem

### 🏆 Quality Guarantee
- **Elite Team**: Former employees of major security firms, certified professionals (OSCP, CISSP, etc.)
- **CISO Oversight**: Every engagement supervised by experienced Chief Information Security Officer
- **Industry Standards**: Following OWASP, NIST, and PTES methodologies
- **Peer Review**: All findings validated by multiple specialists
- **Project Management Team** coordinating engagements and deliverables
- **Report Writing Specialists** delivering clear, actionable documentation

### 1. Application
Organizations interested in our services should send an email with the subject line **[nthw-42]** to [miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com](mailto:miblak@notthehiddenwiki.com)

### 2. Initial Assessment
Our team reviews the application and determines the scope and approach most suitable for the organization's needs.
