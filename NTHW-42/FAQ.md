### 🔍 General Questions

**Q: What is NTHW-42 Unit?**  
A: NTHW-42 Unit is a free professional security testing initiative that brings together elite cybersecurity specialists to provide world-class penetration testing and red team operations at zero cost. Our mission is to make cybersecurity accessible to all organizations, regardless of budget constraints.

**Q: Why do you provide free security testing?**  
A: We believe cybersecurity should be a standard, not a commodity. With increasing regulatory pressure from frameworks like NIS2 and DORA, organizations need professional security testing but often face prohibitive costs. By providing free services, we democratize access to cybersecurity expertise and strengthen the overall digital ecosystem.

**Q: Is there really no cost involved?**  
A: Absolutely none. Our services are completely free of charge. There are no hidden fees, subscription costs, or future payment obligations. The only thing we ask for in return is the opportunity to provide professional references for our team members.

### 🏆 Quality Assurance

**Q: How do you ensure quality without charging fees?**  
A: Our quality is guaranteed through multiple mechanisms:
- **Elite Team Selection**: Only proven professionals with extensive industry backgrounds join our unit
- **CISO Oversight**: Every engagement is supervised by an experienced Chief Information Security Officer
- **Peer Review Process**: All findings undergo rigorous validation by multiple team members
- **Standardized Methodologies**: We follow industry-leading frameworks (OWASP, NIST, PTES)
- **Professional Reputation**: Our team members' careers depend on delivering exceptional results

**Q: What qualifications do your team members have?**  
A: Our specialists include:
- **Certified professionals** (OSCP, OSWE, CRTO, CPTS, MCRTA, CBBH, etc.)
- **Industry veterans** with 5-15+ years of experience
- **Former employees** of major cybersecurity firms
- **Active researchers** and conference speakers
- **CTF champions** and competition winners
- **Open-source contributors** to security tools and frameworks

**Q: How do you maintain consistency across different engagements?**  
A: Through:
- **Standardized Methodologies**: All testing follows established frameworks
- **Quality Checklists**: Comprehensive verification procedures for each engagement phase
- **Executive Review**: CISO-level oversight ensures strategic alignment and quality
- **Team Calibration**: Regular training sessions to maintain consistent standards
- **Client Feedback Integration**: Continuous improvement based on engagement outcomes

### 🔒 Security & Confidentiality

**Q: What about Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?**  
A: 
- The basis for commencing our activities is the acceptance of our terms and conditions. If you wish to sign an NDA, please contact us by email.
- **Confidentiality** is paramount - no client information is ever shared or disclosed
- **Data Handling** follows strict protocols with secure deletion after engagement completion
- **Team Briefings** emphasize confidentiality requirements for all participants
- **Legal Compliance** ensures all privacy and data protection regulations are met

**Q: How do you handle sensitive data during testing?**  
A:
- **Minimal Data Collection**: Only what's necessary for security assessment
- **Secure Storage**: Temporary data stored using enterprise-grade security measures
- **Immediate Deletion**: All client data removed after engagement completion
- **Access Controls**: Strict need-to-know basis for team member data access

**Q: What happens to vulnerabilities discovered during testing?**  
A:
- **Immediate Notification**: Critical vulnerabilities reported immediately upon finding
- **Detailed Documentation**: Comprehensive technical analysis and evidence
- **Remediation Guidance**: Step-by-step instructions for vulnerability resolution
- **Re-testing Offers**: Free verification testing after remediation attempts
- **No Public Disclosure**: Findings remain confidential to the client organization
